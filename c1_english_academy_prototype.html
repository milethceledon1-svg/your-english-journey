GRAMMAR LAB · C1

Inversion for emphasis

En registros formales, ciertas expresiones negativas o restrictivas pueden ir al principio y provocar inversión entre auxiliar y sujeto.

Normal: I had never seen such a convincing argument.

Inversion: Never had I seen such a convincing argument.

Pattern: Negative/restrictive phrase + auxiliary + subject + main verb.

READING · B2+/C1

The Cost of Convenience

When a small city introduced a same-day delivery service, residents initially welcomed the change. It was undeniably convenient: forgotten groceries, books and household essentials could arrive within hours. Yet, as demand grew, the environmental and social costs became harder to overlook. Delivery workers faced increasingly tight schedules, while the number of vehicles on residential streets rose sharply.

Rather than abandoning the service altogether, the city council introduced shared delivery points and encouraged customers to choose flexible time slots. The measures did not solve every problem, but they mitigated some of the pressure. The experiment highlighted a broader lesson: progress is not simply a matter of making things faster; it also requires weighing convenience against long-term consequences.

Comprehension check

What is the main idea of the story?

Vocabulary to save

to overlook = no darse cuenta de / pasar por alto · to weigh = sopesar · consequences = consecuencias

SPEAKING STUDIO

Speak with clarity and precision

Tema de hoy: balancing convenience and responsibility.

Speaking challenge · 60–90 seconds

“Do you think modern technology makes our lives better overall? Explain your view, acknowledge a counterargument, and give a real or hypothetical example.”

  1. State your position clearly.
  2. Use a linking phrase: That said… / To some extent… / A key consideration is…
  3. Include one specific example.
  4. Conclude with a balanced summary.

La grabación depende de los permisos del navegador. Este prototipo no puntúa automáticamente el audio.

Para una evaluación real se debe integrar reconocimiento de voz y análisis de pronunciación, ritmo, fluidez y claridad. Una transcripción por sí sola no mide de forma fiable todos esos aspectos.
